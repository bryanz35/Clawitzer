01 / Connect

Arm connection

Disconnected

Connect the controller by USB, then select its serial port. Opening a port may reset the controller and clear its reference.

Requires desktop Chrome or Edge with Web Serial, on localhost or a secure origin. USB connects directly to this computer; no phone relay is used.

02 / Prepare

Reference before motion

Not referenced

Normal motion requires a complete, saved valid calibration (shipped in firmware). The controller references itself at power-up when the motor shaft still reads the angle recorded at the last marked-home reference. Otherwise — after a stop mid-motion, an e-stop or a fault — disable outputs, manually align all axes to their generated home marks and confirm here.

Referencing starts firmware-controlled homing. This panel never enters bench mode. Exiting an existing bench session discards its unsaved draft after confirmation.

03 / Move

Five joints. Hold to jog.

Motion unavailable

Hold a joint’s − or + button with the mouse, or hold Shift plus a direction key. You can also hold Enter on a focused jog button. Releasing the input or leaving this window requests a hold at the latest firmware-reported pose and stops streaming. Motion never resumes automatically.
